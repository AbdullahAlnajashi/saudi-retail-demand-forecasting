Retail Demand Decision Support

Google Trends search interest (index 0-100) as a demand proxy, Saudi Arabia, monthly 2015-2026. Interest is NOT unit sales. Indices of all 5 products share one common scale. IS 366 - Group Project 1.

Product:

1. History and 12-month forecast
Black = actual history. Red = forecast (Nov 2026 - Oct 2027). Shaded = 80% range.
2. Seasonality: which months are strongest
1.0 = average month. Above 1.0 = busier than normal.
3. Accuracy check: last 12 months (not used for training)
Actual vs best model vs simple baseline (same month last year).
4. Annual average interest, all products
Shows long-term growth and relative size.
5. Model comparison (3 rolling test years, 36 months)
Lower is better. MAE/RMSE in index points. MAPE shown only where volume is high enough (n/a otherwise). Selected = lowest MAE.

Method: models trained on Jan 2015 onward; tested on the last three 12-month windows. Forecast ranges use backtest RMSE (approximate). Assumes past patterns continue; search interest can differ from actual sales.
